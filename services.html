Common questions about working with us

Real answers to what clients actually want to know before starting.

How quickly can we see something working?

Most teams have a usable solution within 4-6 weeks. We start with discovery (1 week), build the first working version (2-3 weeks), then iterate based on actual use. You are not waiting months for results. We get something live fast and improve from there.

Do we need to buy your products to use your services?

No. Services can work with your existing tools. If NyLi products make sense for your situation, we will show you exactly how they fit. We often help teams optimize what they already have before adding anything new.

What is required from our side to get started?

Three things: someone who owns the problem and can make decisions, access to the systems we will be working with, and 2-3 hours per week from your subject matter experts during implementation. We handle the heavy lifting.

What if we have already tried consultants and it did not stick?

Most consulting fails because it delivers recommendations, not working systems. We build actual solutions you can use on Monday morning. We also stay engaged to ensure adoption actually happens. No 200-page PDFs that collect dust.

How do we handle pricing and budgeting?

We work with milestone-based pricing so you know what you are getting and what it costs upfront. During our initial consultation, we discuss your budget range and design an approach that delivers maximum value within your constraints.

Can you work with our mess of existing systems?

That is exactly what we do. Most clients have 5-10 systems that do not talk to each other. We are experts at connecting Salesforce to Zoho to Excel to whatever else you are using. The messier it is, the more value we can add.

What if we are not ready for AI?

Perfect. Most teams need to organize their knowledge and fix their processes before AI makes sense. We help you build the foundation first. AI works best when it has clean data and clear workflows to support.

Who typically owns this on the client side?

Usually it is a Director or VP of Operations, IT, or Sales who is tired of band-aid solutions. Sometimes it is a founder who knows things are broken but does not have time to fix them. You need someone with budget authority and the ability to get 2-3 team members involved.

What happens after the engagement ends?

You own everything we build, including documentation and training materials. Most clients can operate independently after our engagement. Some keep us on retainer for quarterly optimization, but that is optional. We build for self-sufficiency.

How are you different from traditional consultants?

We are builders, not advisors. Traditional consultants deliver strategy decks. We deliver working systems. Think of us as your temporary operations team that builds something sustainable, then hands it off cleanly.

What size companies do you typically work with?

Our sweet spot is 20-200 person organizations that have outgrown startup chaos but are not big enough for dedicated operations teams. We also work with larger companies on specific departmental solutions. If you are feeling operational pain from growth, we are probably a fit.

Can you help us choose between different platforms?

Yes, and we will be honest about it. We are certified in multiple platforms, including Zoho, Salesforce, and HubSpot, but we are not married to any of them. We assess based on your needs, budget, and team capability.

What if we just need one specific thing fixed?

That is fine. We do targeted implementations all the time. Fix reporting, organize your knowledge base, automate follow-ups, or solve whatever is causing the most pain. Start there and expand only if it makes sense.

How do you ensure knowledge transfer?

Every engagement includes documentation, training, and handoff. We record key processes, create operating guides, and run working sessions with your team. The goal is for you to own and operate everything confidently after we are done.

What is your typical engagement timeline?

Discovery takes about a week. First implementation milestone is usually 3-4 weeks after that. Full rollout with adoption support runs 6-12 weeks total. We can move faster or slower based on urgency and team availability.
