Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really deploy in under 5 minutes?

Yes. Copy a single line of JavaScript, paste it into your website, and your branded chat assistant is live. Full configuration and customization can be done through the web interface without touching code again.

How does multi-tenant management work?

The Owner Portal lets you create and manage unlimited tenant accounts. Each tenant gets their own isolated bot instance, knowledge base, conversation history, and team member access. You control feature flags, subscription states, and billing from one central dashboard.

What kind of security and compliance features are included?

NyLi includes JWT-based authentication, role-based access control, intelligent rate limiting, SSL/TLS encryption, audit logging, and environment-based secrets management. All payment data is handled by Stripe, maintaining PCI compliance. Full conversation logs can be exported for compliance reporting.

How does Stripe integration work?

NyLi connects to your Stripe account and automatically handles subscription creation, payment processing, trial periods, upgrades, and cancellations. You set your pricing in the platform, and Stripe handles the rest. Payment data never touches your servers.

What deployment options are available?

NyLi is Docker-native and can deploy to Railway (one-click), Heroku (git push), your own Kubernetes cluster, or any container platform. You own your code and control your infrastructure. No vendor lock-in.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. All new accounts get a free 14-day trial with full access to Professional plan features. No credit card required to start. Test every feature, deploy on your sites, and evaluate real conversations before committing.
